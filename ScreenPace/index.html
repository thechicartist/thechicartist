Good to know

Questions, answered plainly.

Do apps in one rule share the allowance?

Yes. If Instagram and Reddit are together in a five-minute rule, their combined use counts toward those five minutes. Put them in separate rules when they need separate allowances.

Can I change limits during the day?

Yes. You can add new rules as needed and pause limits to edit rules or Focus Hours, then resume immediately or wait for the automatic resume at the next hour.

Can the developer see which apps I choose?

No. Your choices are handled on device through Apple's privacy-preserving Screen Time frameworks and are never uploaded to ScreenPace or The Chic Artist.

Is this parental-control software?

No. ScreenPace requests individual authorization and is designed for anyone designing their own digital wellness routine.

Why not use a daily limit?

A daily limit can disappear before the morning is over. An hourly allowance creates many smaller chances to reset, so one unplanned scroll does not define the rest of your day.
