Privacy Policy

ScreenPace is built around a simple rule: your screen habits belong to you and stay on your device.

Last updated: August 18, 2026

Short version: ScreenPace has no accounts, cloud service, advertising, or remote analytics. It does not transmit your app selections, rules, usage thresholds, schedules, or written override reasons to the developer.

1. Overview

ScreenPace (the “App”) is developed and published by The Chic Artist (“we,” “us,” or “our”). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App accesses, how that information is used, and what leaves your device.

ScreenPace is a self-control tool. It is not a remote monitoring service and does not provide a parent, employer, or any other person with access to your activity.

2. Information stored on your device

To provide its features, ScreenPace stores the following information locally:

  • Your ScreenPace rules and their names.
  • Apple-provided selection tokens representing the apps, categories, or websites you choose.
  • Your Focus Hours schedule and the allowances assigned to each rule.
  • Whether limits are active or paused, including a planned resume time.
  • Written reasons you enter when pausing or ending limits.
  • Private Insights derived from those locally stored override events.

Rules and enforcement state are stored in the on-device App Group shared only by ScreenPace and its embedded Screen Time extension. Written override reasons are stored separately in the main app's private local container and are not shared with the extension.

3. Screen Time access

ScreenPace requests Apple's individual Family Controls authorization. It uses Apple's Family Controls, Device Activity, and Managed Settings frameworks only to let you select distractions, observe allowance thresholds, and apply shields on this device.

Apple's privacy-preserving picker supplies opaque selection tokens. ScreenPace uses those tokens to configure your limits and does not upload them. ScreenPace does not receive a remote feed of your general device activity or browsing history.

4. Information we do not collect

ScreenPace does not require an account and does not collect, receive, store on a server, or have access to:

  • Your name, email address, contacts, or location.
  • The apps, categories, or websites you choose to limit.
  • Your device-wide Screen Time history.
  • Your written override reasons or Private Insights.
  • Advertising identifiers, product analytics, crash analytics, or tracking data.
  • Payment or billing information.

The App contains no third-party analytics SDKs, advertising networks, tracking pixels, or developer-operated backend service.

Purchases

If you purchase ScreenPace, Apple processes the transaction through the App Store. We do not receive your card number, billing address, or other payment credentials. Apple's handling of App Store transactions is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, disclose, or share your information with third parties because ScreenPace does not transmit that information to us in the first place.

5. Retention, deletion, and your control

Written override history is retained locally for up to 90 days. You can clear it at any time from Private Insights inside ScreenPace. Rules and schedules can be changed or deleted when limits are paused or inactive.

Deleting ScreenPace removes its locally stored settings and private history from your device. We cannot recover deleted information because we never receive a copy.

6. Security

ScreenPace relies on iOS sandboxing, App Group access controls, and your device security features such as its passcode, Face ID, or Touch ID. No method of storage is guaranteed to be perfect, but keeping this information on your device avoids the additional risks of a developer-operated account or cloud database.

7. Children's privacy

ScreenPace is designed for people setting limits for themselves and is not a parental monitoring service. The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children—or from any other user.

8. Changes to this policy

If ScreenPace's privacy practices change, we will update this page and its “Last updated” date before the new practices take effect. Material changes will also be reflected in the App Store privacy information and, when appropriate, inside the App.

9. Contact us

For privacy questions, email thechicartist@thechicartist.com.
