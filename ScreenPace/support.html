Frequently asked questions

Get ScreenPace back in rhythm.

How does an hourly allowance work?

Each active rule receives its configured allowance at the top of the hour. When selected activity reaches that threshold, iOS applies a shield until the next hourly interval starts.

When do apps in different rules block?

Each rule is monitored independently. For example, apps in a five-minute rule block when their combined use reaches five minutes, while apps in a separate ten-minute rule remain available until that rule reaches ten minutes.

Why did several apps block together?

Apps, categories, and websites selected in the same rule share one allowance. Create separate rules when you want separate counters.

What happens when two rules overlap?

The rules remain independent. If the same app is selected in two rules, the stricter active result wins because a shield from either rule can keep the app blocked.

How do Focus Hours work?

Every rule can have one allowance during Focus Hours and another outside them. Focus Hours use whole-hour boundaries so the change stays aligned with the hourly reset. Weekday and overnight schedules are supported.

Can I edit a rule while limits are active?

Yes. You can add or edit rules while limits are active, and ScreenPace keeps usage already accumulated during the current hour. Pause or end limits only when you need to change Focus Hours.

How do I resume after pausing?

A pause automatically ends at the next hour. You can also tap “Resume Limits Now” for a fresh hourly allowance. The app refreshes its status at the boundary and whenever it returns to the foreground.

Why does ScreenPace ask for a written reason?

The short reflection creates a deliberate moment before removing a limit. Your reason is stored only on your iPhone, appears in Private Insights, is retained for up to 90 days, and can be cleared whenever you want.

Screen Time access is denied. What should I do?

Open the iPhone Settings app, find ScreenPace, and review its Screen Time or Family Controls permission. Return to ScreenPace and tap “Allow Screen Time Access” again. If authorization remains unavailable, restart the iPhone and try once more.

A limit did not trigger at the exact second. Is that expected?

Yes. Apple's Device Activity system manages threshold delivery in the background, so it may not fire to the exact second. Background audio or video activity can also count toward a selected rule.

Will ScreenPace work after I restart my iPhone?

Monitoring is designed to remain registered. Open ScreenPace after a restart to confirm “Limits active.” If it reports that monitoring needs attention, verify Screen Time access and start the limits again.

Does ScreenPace upload my app choices or reasons?

No. ScreenPace has no account, cloud service, advertising, or analytics. Rules stay in its on-device App Group and written reasons stay in the main app's private container.

How do I request a refund?

Apple processes App Store purchases and refund requests. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com and follow Apple's instructions.
