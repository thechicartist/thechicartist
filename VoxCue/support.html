Frequently asked questions

How does the free trial work?

VoxCue is free to use in full for 7 days from the moment you first open the app. No account or credit card is required to start your trial. After 7 days, you can unlock VoxCue forever with a single one-time purchase of $4.99 — there are no subscriptions or recurring charges.

Does VoxCue require an internet connection?

No. VoxCue works entirely offline. Your scripts, recordings, and edits are stored only on your device — nothing is uploaded to a server or cloud service.

How does voice-activated scrolling work?

VoxCue uses on-device speech recognition to detect your speaking pace and scrolls your script to keep up with you in real time. If you'd rather control the pace yourself, you can switch to manual scroll mode at any time in settings.

Can I record with the front camera too?

VoxCue is built around back-camera, landscape recording for sharper, more professional footage — ideal for YouTube videos and long-form content.

What happens to my videos after I record?

Your recordings are saved directly to VoxCue's in-app gallery and to your photo library on your device. You can trim, edit, and share them directly from the app — they never leave your device unless you choose to share them.

I purchased VoxCue but it's showing as locked on a new device. What do I do?

Open VoxCue, go to the unlock screen, and tap "Restore Purchase." This restores your one-time purchase using your Apple ID at no additional cost.

How do I request a refund?

All purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. To request a refund, visit reportaproblem.apple.com and follow Apple's refund process.

I found a bug or have a feature request.

We'd love to hear from you — email thechicartist@thechicartist.com with as much detail as possible, including your device model and iOS version, and we'll take a look.
