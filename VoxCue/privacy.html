Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 14, 2026

VoxCue ("the App") is developed and published by Neetika Agarwal ("we," "us," or "our"). The App is designed around a simple principle: your videos, your scripts, and your voice belong to you, and they stay on your device. This Privacy Policy explains what information VoxCue accesses, how it's used, and what — if anything — ever leaves your device.

1. Information we do not collect

VoxCue does not require you to create an account, and we do not collect, store, or have access to any of the following:

  • Your name, email address, or contact information
  • Your video recordings, scripts, or any content you create in the App
  • Audio recorded for speech-recognition / voice-activated scrolling
  • Your location
  • Analytics, usage tracking, or advertising identifiers

VoxCue does not contain third-party analytics SDKs, advertising networks, or tracking pixels.

2. On-device processing

VoxCue uses your device's microphone and on-device speech recognition to power voice-activated scrolling. This processing happens entirely on your device using Apple's on-device frameworks. Audio used for this purpose is not recorded, stored, transmitted, or sent to VoxCue, Apple, or any third party as part of this feature.

3. Camera and microphone access

VoxCue requests access to your device's camera and microphone solely to allow you to record video within the App. Recordings are saved locally to the App's storage and, if you choose, to your device's photo library. VoxCue does not upload recordings to any server.

4. Photo library access

If you choose to save or share a recording, VoxCue may request access to your photo library to save the file or to access existing videos you select for editing. This access is used only for the action you initiate and is not used to scan, index, or transmit your existing photo library contents.

5. Purchases

VoxCue offers a one-time in-app purchase to unlock the App after the free trial period. Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. VoxCue does not receive or store your payment information, billing address, or card details — this is handled solely by Apple in accordance with Apple's Privacy Policy.

6. Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your information with third parties, because we do not collect any information to share in the first place.

7. Children's privacy

VoxCue is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from children. Because VoxCue does not collect personal information from any user, this applies equally to users of all ages.

8. Data security

Because all content created in VoxCue is stored locally on your device, the security of that content is governed by your device's own security features (such as passcode, Face ID, or Touch ID) and iOS sandboxing protections.

9. Your control over your data

You can delete any recording, script, or piece of content created in VoxCue at any time directly within the App. Deleting the App from your device removes all associated local data, except for any recordings you separately saved to your photo library.

10. Changes to this policy

If we make changes to this Privacy Policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of VoxCue after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at thechicartist@thechicartist.com.
