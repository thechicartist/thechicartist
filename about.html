Paint Beautiful Watercolor Florals with Confidence

Through YouTube tutorials, beginner-friendly eBooks, and online classes, I make watercolor approachable, enjoyable, and inspiring for artists of every level.

Where Creativity Begins...

Hi, I'm Neetika—a watercolor artist, educator, and lifelong believer that anyone can learn to paint with the right guidance.

What started as a love for watercolor florals has grown into a passion for teaching thousands of aspiring artists around the world. I know how overwhelming watercolor can feel at first, which is why my mission is to make it approachable, enjoyable, and inspiring for every beginner. Through simple techniques and step-by-step lessons, I help students build confidence and discover the joy of painting.

My teaching is rooted in the belief that watercolor doesn't have to be intimidating. Whether you're picking up a brush for the very first time or looking to refine your loose floral style, I'm here to guide you every step of the way. On my YouTube channel, in my eBooks, and through my online classes, you'll find practical lessons designed to help you create beautiful paintings while enjoying the creative process.

For me, watercolor is more than just a medium—it's a way to slow down, express yourself, and find beauty in everyday moments. Every lesson I create is crafted with the hope that you'll not only learn new techniques but also fall in love with painting.

I'm so glad you're here. Grab your brushes, make yourself comfortable, and let's create something beautiful together.
