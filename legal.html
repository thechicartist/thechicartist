1. Privacy by Design

VoxCue is designed from the ground up to respect your absolute privacy. We firmly believe that your speech data, scripts, and video recordings belong solely to you.

2. On-Device Speech Processing

All voice recognition, transcription, and tracking calculations are performed entirely locally on your Apple device utilizing iOS core framework technologies. Audio files or streams are never sent to external servers, processed by remote third parties, or collected by us.

3. Camera and Microphone Access

VoxCue requires explicit system permissions to access your device's microphone and camera. These permissions are strictly utilized to enable real-time teleprompter tracking and to record your performance directly into your local photo gallery stack. We do not transmit, review, or store this data.

4. Data Storage

Any scripts, settings, or videos generated within the app are stored exclusively on your local device storage or within your private iCloud container if enabled. We maintain no server databases and have no technical capability to view or modify your data.

5. Analytics & Crash Data

We may collect anonymized, aggregate telemetry data or hardware crash reports provided strictly through Apple's developer consoles (App Store Connect) to help maintain app stability. This information cannot be linked back to you personally.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding our privacy standards, contact us directly at: thechicartist@thechicartist.com
